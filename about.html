About Us

Have you written the hit song and need a booking agency to play your shows?

Michael Carty Bookings Agency and Artist Management is the only place to look. Having worked in the field for many years, we are experts at managing elite performers.

Our committed staff is here to help you with all facets of your music career — arranging flights and venues, legal paperwork, hospitality riders, and technical riders. We take care of all booking and administrative duties so you can concentrate on what you do best: make music.

We are artist-first by design. Careers are built over years, not weekends, and the right opportunities compound. That is the work we do.

Mission

Connect exceptional artists with exceptional opportunities.

We represent distinctive talent and pair them with the stages, audiences, and partners that move careers forward — with the admin, logistics, and strategy handled quietly in the background.

Values

Artist-first. Long-term thinking. Clear communication. No wasted motion.

Let's build something that lasts.

Whether you're an artist ready for representation or a promoter planning a lineup, we'd like to hear from you.
