Kompa

Jeon

JEON (born Jonathan Thiel, Jan 27 1991) is a celebrated Aruban singer, songwriter, and producer from Santa Cruz, Aruba. Trained on piano, bass, cuatro, wiri, and conga, he blends Caribbean roots with Latin pop.

He gained regional fame with his 2016 single "Dushi Bida" and achieved international stardom in 2018 co-writing and featuring on J Balvin & Anitta's global hit "Machika." He's also credited on Nicky Jam & J Balvin's "X (Equis)," and has opened for Chris Brown, Don Omar, and Juan Luis Guerra.

R&B

Essovilla

Essovilla is an Aruban DJ, event organizer, and founder of the Caribbean party brand Caribang ("Caribbean Club Bangers"). He took his name from the Aruban neighborhood where he grew up.

He started with Caribang mixtapes and grew it into a major event movement hosting massive parties and festival takeovers in Aruba and the Netherlands (Den Haag) and beyond. Known for high-energy sets blending Caribbean, Afro, and Latin vibes — billed as "The Boss" — he regularly tours destination events, club nights, and Carnaval, bridging the Dutch Caribbean scene between Aruba and Europe.

Kompa

Issabeach

Issabeach (styled Issa Beach) is a popular Aruban cultural personality, social media presence, and culinary/entertainment figure. He collaborates with local artists like Vale Croes and Jeon.

As a recording contributor in Caribbean sounds, he's featured on "Sak Pa Bo Lora" with Jeon and "Krokodil" with Rincon Boyz. He also runs the Issa Beach Food Truck in Paradera, Aruba — loaded fries, wings, and burgers — which built a strong local following after opening during the pandemic.

Caribbean sounds, island vibes, and global music styles

JRalph

JRALPH (Jralph Music) is a versatile musician, producer, songwriter, singer, and mixing/mastering engineer raised in Aruba after moving from Haiti at age 11. By 13 he was already writing and producing his own music.

Born in Haiti and relocated to Aruba at 11, he learned multiple instruments, studied music theory, and explores Caribbean and international genres. He blends Creole, Papiamento, Reggaeton, and Dancehall, and runs his own production, mixing, and mastering work. Notable releases include the EP Summa Ting (with Dreyh and Ataniro) and singles "Un Rato" and "Carnival Comet," and he performs at events like Windsong Fest.

Prominent Aruban DJ and music personality

DJ Whiteboy

DJ Whiteboy (born Rayen Geerman, July 1979) is a prominent Aruban DJ and music personality, born and raised in Aruba. He's a fixture in the Aruban nightlife, festival, and concert scene.

Performing as DJ Whiteboy (also known online via Aruba Finest), he regularly plays alongside local bands, artists, and fellow DJs at major events like the Flip Flop Festival, the San Nicolas Soca Edition street concerts, Caribbean nights at The Office Aruba, and brunch afterparties.

Multi-talented open-format DJ

BB Bad

Bianca "BB Bad" Dijkhoffz is a Sint Maarten-born DJ, professional dancer, and digital influencer blending Caribbean influences with global club sounds.

Born and raised in Sint Maarten, she developed a passion for dance and Caribbean culture before working internationally, including a stint in North Hollywood. Transitioning from professional dancer and choreographer — performing on high-profile visual and dance projects — into a globally touring open-format DJ, BB Bad amasses a strong digital presence across YouTube and Instagram. She blends Caribbean musical influences with global club sounds, touring internationally and appearing across media platforms.

Variation of Local & Latin Music

Shine Music

ShineMusic (ShineMusic Official Aruba) is a prominent Aruban musical group and collective led by musical director and bassist Reggie Curie. They work with local vocalists across Caribbean, Soca, and Kompa sounds.

Notable projects include the album Kompa Time, with collaborative singles "Jij en Ik" (J-Rich Jerrich), "No Ta Facil" (SidSim Sidney), and "Kriye Sou Mwen" (ChicoBenz Ebenson), all produced by Reggie Curie. They perform regularly during Aruba's Carnival season, backing artists alongside groups like TOB Carnival Group, N Fuzion Music, and Groove Masters, and provide award-winning arrangements for Himno y Bandera events.

Kompa

Clarence

Clearance Arduin, known as Clarence, is a Caribbean artist born in The Netherlands and now based in Curaçao. He specializes in Kompa, turning real emotions into melodies.

His sound is rooted in love, passion, and real emotions, transforming everyday stories into music listeners can feel. Clarence's mission is simple: turn feelings into melodies and create music that speaks from the heart — love, passion, real emotions, music.
